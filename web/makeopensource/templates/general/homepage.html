Welcome to MakeOpenSource!

We are the official student-led open-source programming club within the School of Engineering and Applied Sciences (SEAS) at the University at Buffalo (UB). Our goal is to create a community of programmers and developers (of any skill level) who can collaborate and contribute to open-source projects. The club is open to both undergraduate and graduate students. Check out some of our projects and join our Discord server to get involved!
